GRACE (Gauging Resilience in the face of uncertAinty through Creative Engagement) has been funded by the Economic and Social Research Council (ESRC), part of UK Research and Innovation, under grant reference UKRI6755.

Employers increasingly look for resilience in the face of uncertainty, yet little is known about how this capacity develops in individuals. GRACE asks whether creative practice provides conditions for that development, and whether the change can be observed in how people act and move rather than inferred from questionnaire scores.

The project brings together behavioural science, creative practice and computer vision, led by Professor Gaëlle Vallée-Tourangeau, Professor Marcus O'Dair and Professor Dimitrios Makris. Between October 2026 and March 2027 it runs three workshops, a studio pilot and a synthesis event that will set out a research agenda for a larger programme.
