        The GRACE network brings together researchers and practitioners in behavioural science, the creative arts, coaching and computer vision who are interested in how people learn to work with uncertainty.

        - invitations to GRACE workshops and events;
        - the workshop reports and other project outputs as soon as they are available;
        - the opportunity to contribute to reports, with each contribution credited using [CRediT roles](https://credit.niso.org/);
        - news of the follow-on research programme and opportunities to take part in it.

        To join, use the form below. In your message, please tell us your role and organisation, your discipline or practice, and what draws you to the question of resilience in the face of uncertainty.

        We use your details only to send GRACE news and invitations. You can leave the network at any time by emailing [grace-network@kingston.ac.uk](mailto:grace-network@kingston.ac.uk). For any other question about the project, please write to the same address.
