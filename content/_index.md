        Gauging Resilience in the face of uncertAinty through Creative Engagement (GRACE)
      image:
        filename: grace-hero.png
      text: |
        Employers rank resilience in the face of uncertainty as the fastest-growing skill they need ([WEF, 2025](https://reports.weforum.org/docs/WEF_Future_of_Jobs_Report_2025.pdf)), yet little is known about how individuals develop it. Research usually treats tolerance of uncertainty as a stable trait, measured by questionnaire.

        GRACE asks whether creative practice, with its open-ended problems and no predetermined answers, helps people become more resilient, and whether that change can be seen in behaviour as it happens. We expect it to show as less rush to settle on an answer and more ability to re-frame problems.

        The project brings together behavioural science, the creative arts and computer vision, using video analysis to track movement and gesture in a studio. Three workshops, a pilot study and a synthesis event will test the approach and shape a larger research programme.
