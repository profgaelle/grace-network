Heather Urquhart is an improviser, performer, director and facilitator, and co-founder of AndAlso Improv. Her creative practice spans theatrical and musical improvisation, blending play, storytelling, spontaneity and collaboration. She works across performance, education and applied improvisation, exploring how creativity can help people navigate uncertainty, embrace possibility and connect with one another. Her work is rooted in the belief that play is a powerful tool for learning, discovery and human connection.
