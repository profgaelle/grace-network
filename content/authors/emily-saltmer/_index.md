Emily is the founder of Mighty Coaching and an accredited Executive Coach, Coach Supervisor, and Leadership Facilitator with over 20 years’ experience in senior talent and leadership development roles within global organisations.

Drawing on psychology, physiology, and neuroscience, she takes a human-systemic approach to coaching and development. Emily integrates creative methodologies such as LEGO® Serious Play®, Constellations, Metaphor, and nature-based practices to help clients uncover blind spots, access deeper insights, and expand their thinking. Her work supports clients in improving self-awareness, clarity, confidence, resilience, decision-making, and fresh perspectives on complex challenges.
