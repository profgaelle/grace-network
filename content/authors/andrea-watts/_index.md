Andréa Watts founded her creative coaching business UnglueYou® in 2012. Having developed the Collage Coaching Technique™ (CCT), she is recognised as a pioneer in arts-based coaching. She delivers ICF-accredited training for coaches and has guest lectured about the ‘Science Beside the Art’ of the technique for Coaching and Coach Psychology programmes at several universities, including Cambridge. Andréa authored *Collage as a Creative Coaching Tool*, contributed to *Arts-Based Coaching*, and is working on *The Collage Coaching Technique™*, all with Routledge.
