Marcus O'Dair leads the creative practice strand of GRACE. He uses creative methods such as improvisation, photography and collage in coaching and leadership development, including British Council programmes in Mexico and West Africa. His forthcoming book *Leading with Creativity* (Cambridge University Press) sets out a framework for creative leadership under uncertainty.
